How to use this page

What each tab answers

Every fleet card has these tabs, answered for the book picked above them. Each one has a How this is worked out fold under its headline for the detail.

Arrivals home
Units whose diagram ends at the home depot, split into before noon, the afternoon and evening, and after midnight. One that calls in and goes out again is not counted.
Home before 8pm
Diagrams that end at the home depot between noon and 20:00 — the units a late shift can start on — and when each goes out again.
Restricted units
A restricted unit may never run on its own, so it can only take a diagram that is coupled on every leg. These are those diagrams, and whether a unit on them can stay on that work night after night.
Days back to depot
For every place a unit can be left: how many days the diagrams take to bring it home, and which diagrams do it.
Mileage
Miles per unit a day and a year, by sub-fleet, divided by the units the depot owns. Always the whole week, whichever book is picked.
Attendable stands
Every place a unit stands still for two hours or more during the day — long enough for somebody to get to it.
Cannot contain
Places a restricted unit cannot be worked from, because every diagram out of there leaves it running alone at some point.
Together AM, apart PM
Units that go out coupled, are put away together, and only come apart later — so neither is free until the afternoon.
Place codes
What each nine-character place code in the prints means.

If something looks wrong

A fleet has no card
None of the prints dropped has a diagram for that fleet. Check all four books were dropped — it may be in the one that was left out.
“The plan needs more units than the fleet owns”
Either the units owned is set too low, or the prints give more than one sub-fleet the same label. Both are set under Fleets & depots.
A place shows as a raw code
The tool has no name for it yet, so it is shown as it is in the prints rather than guessed at. The Place codes tab lists every one still without a name.

What the diagram prints mean for looking after the fleet. Each fleet gets a card that answers maintenance planning’s questions, one tab per question.

  • 1 · The prints Drop the whole set together: the Mon–Thu book (FSX) and the Friday, Saturday and Sunday ones. Each covers days the others don’t, and the answers are worked out across the whole week.
  • 2 · The fleets Check the units owned under Fleets & depots. The prints can’t show a spare unit, and every miles-per-unit figure is divided by it.
  • 3 · The day Pick Mon–Thu, Friday, Saturday or Sunday on any card and every card follows. Mileage is always the whole week.
  • 4 · What you get A card per fleet. Save this fleet puts its answers in a workbook, a tab per question; Save every fleet puts them all in one.

Fleets & depots — units owned, home depot, and where each fleet can be repaired

The prints don’t say any of this, so it is set here. It is remembered on this computer, and the cards redraw as soon as you change something.

Units owned matters most: a unit spare all week never appears in the prints, and every miles-per-unit figure is divided by it. The defaults are the depot’s own figures, under the labels the prints use — 375/6 covers the whole 375/6/7/8 group, and 465/9 covers every 465.
