How to use this page

Quick start

  1. Get the whole set of prints. The Word documents the plan is published as — the Monday-to-Thursday book (FSX) and the Friday, Saturday and Sunday ones. Drop them together: each covers days the others do not, and the answers below are worked out over a whole week.
  2. Drop them on the road. Or click it to browse. A text or CSV save of the same document works just as well — the page reads what is inside the file, not what it is called.
  3. Check the fleet sizes. How many units the depot owns is the one thing the prints cannot say, and every mileage per unit is divided by it. They are under Fleets & depots below.
  4. Read a fleet's card. One per fleet, each with its questions on tabs. Save this fleet as a spreadsheet puts the same answers in a workbook, a tab per question.

What it answers

When does the fleet come home?
Every diagram that ends at the home depot, and how many are in before eight in the evening — the ones a late shift can start on.
What can be worked on, and where?
Every place a unit stands still for two hours or more during the day, which is long enough for somebody to get to it.
What can carry a restricted unit?
A restricted unit may never run on its own, so it can only take a diagram that is coupled on every leg. Also where one cannot be contained — a place with no such diagram out of it.
Where do diagrams come apart?
Read off the formation column, the same way the berthing sheets read it, and split into the ones that part on the working they leave on and the ones that go out together and come apart later.
What does a unit run?
Per day, per week and per year, by sub-fleet — divided by the units the depot owns, not by the diagrams, because the spares and the ones on exam take their turn through the same work.

If something looks wrong

A fleet has no card
No diagram in the prints carries that Fleet line. Check the right books were dropped — the fleet may be in a document that was left out.
“The plan needs more diagrams than the fleet has units”
The fleet size is wrong, or the prints label more than one sub-fleet the same way. Both are fixed under Fleets & depots.
A place shows as a raw code
It has no name in the tool yet. It is listed under What the place codes mean, at the foot of each card, and a guess would read as fact — so it is left as it came.

Fleets & depots — how many units each sub-fleet has, which depot owns it, and where it can be repaired

These are your arrangements, not something the prints state, so they are set here rather than waiting on a new version of this file. They are remembered on this computer, and every card redraws as soon as you change one.

Fleet size is the one figure the prints cannot supply — a unit standing spare all week never appears in one — and every mileage per unit is divided by it. The numbers that ship are the depot's own, keyed to the labels the prints use: 375/6 is the 375/6/7/8 group, and 465/9 is every 465 diagram, because that is how the prints label them.
