Fleet & depot · unit berthing

Berthing sheets the week's books, built from the plans

Drop the week's planning paperwork on the matching panel below and the unit berthing books come back built — checked on screen, then saved. Nothing leaves this computer.

Quick start

  1. Get the paperwork as files. For a weekday: the Diagram Summary and Diagram Detail reports for the date — from Genius as PDFs, or from Integrale as the two CSV exports. For the weekend: the diagram prints Word document(s), or the same text. In Genius, a Control Cycle must be created before the Diagram Summary and Diagram Detail reports can be run — and if the Metro and High Speed sheets are wanted, Control Cycles must be created for those fleets too. Check this before you export In Genius, File › Session Settings — tick “Show diagram sections on the diagram summary report”. Without it you only get the AM unit positions. Tick it and you get the PM positions too — which is what puts afternoon formations the right way round, the Grove Park PM departures especially. Leave it unticked and the books still build, but every formation made up during the day is printed in its morning order, and it cannot be put right afterwards. The review list will tell you it happened.
    Where to find the reports in Genius In Genius: Diagrams → Summary Report… then Diagrams → Detail Report… — save each as .pdf for the date. First check File › Session Settings has “Show diagram sections on the diagram summary report” ticked — without it the Summary carries the AM unit positions only, not the PM ones.
    Getting the two CSVs out of Integrale First, the Diagram Summary
    1. Open the Stock Diagrams list and click [GO→] without selecting a filter.
    2. Click [Export To Excel].
    3. Save it in a sensible folder under a sensible name, with File Type set to .csv, then click [Save].
    Then the Diagram Detail
    1. Open the Stock Diagrams list and click [GO→] again, still without a filter.
    2. Click Stock Diagram Detail Report on the toolbar.
    3. The dialog opens at the bottom of the screen — click […] under Output Path.
    4. Pick the same folder as the Summary, give it a sensible name, File Type .csv again, then [Save].
    5. Click [OK] — you may have to scroll down to see it. It takes a minute or so, then tells you it is done.
    That is the pair. Drop them both on the weekday panel below.
  2. Drop them on the matching panel below — the weekday reports on the first panel, the weekend prints on the second. Or click a panel to browse for the files. On a machine that will not let the files be saved anywhere the browser can reach, either panel will take the paperwork pasted in instead — the weekday CSVs into their two boxes, the weekend prints into theirs. The one thing that cannot be pasted is a PDF: there is no text in one to copy.
  3. Look the sheets over on screen, then save. Every build shows a preview and a review list of anything that needs a human eye before the books go out. The books come out ready for the printer — A4 portrait, sized so all eight columns land on one page across, and broken so no location is cut in half. The preview marks where each page starts, so you can see it before you save.
Weekdays · Mon – Fri

Weekday sheets from the planning reports

Needs both reports for the date — the Diagram Summary (one line per diagram) and the Diagram Detail (every stop of every diagram) — from Genius as PDFs or CSVs, or from Integrale as its two CSV exports. Drop the pair together; a Monday pair builds the Monday books.

Open each CSV, select all and copy — Notepad or Excel, either reads. Paste each into its own box below: both boxes, both for the same date. Integrale calls the second one Diagrams. Only the CSV exports can be pasted; a PDF has no text to copy, so those still have to be dropped as files.

Headcodes

Only Gillingham, Victoria and Grove Park carry a headcode as standard. Tick a book to put one on every line of it — empty moves and platform starters alike. The books rebuild as you tick, so save them again afterwards.

Platform stands

A unit that stands in a platform for an hour or more has arguably berthed there. It gets a line when the report shunts it on the spot; otherwise it is named on the review list and left off. Tick this to put those on the sheets too.

What the tool does with these reports

It reads every diagram's day from the Detail report, works out where each unit berths, and writes the entries the way the hand-built books do — times, diagram numbers, AM and PM columns, flags, siding notes and end markers, all filled in.

It also leaves things off on purpose: an empty move onto a berth with no passenger work after it is not a line, the same as the hand-built sheets. Every one is named on the review list, so nothing disappears silently.

The full rulebook is on the Rules tab of each book once it is built, and in BERTHING SHEET RULES for circulating. Both are written out from the tool's own tables, so neither can fall behind what the books actually do — which is why they are not repeated here.

If a weekday build looks wrong
"No Diagram Summary rows found" / "No Diagram Detail itineraries found"
One of the two reports is missing or the wrong kind. Both must be dropped, saved for the same date — both from Genius (PDF or CSV, and one of each is fine) or both from Integrale. A Genius report paired with an Integrale export won't build.
A location comes up under its own heading
A unit berthed somewhere the section list doesn't know. It gets its own section in alphabetical order and a note on the review list — check it, and say if it should live under an existing section instead.
A train is missing
Check the review list first: empty moves to a berth are left off deliberately and each one is named there. If it isn't listed, report the diagram number so the rule can be traced.
Weekend · Sat & Sun

Saturday & Sunday sheets from the diagram prints

Needs the weekend diagram prints Word document (.docx or .doc, or the same thing saved as plain text). If reissued prints exist, drop them too — together or in a later drop — and the reissued diagrams replace their originals before anything is built.

Open the prints in Word, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste into the first box. The diagram lines have to keep their tabs, so paste as it comes — do not tidy it up. The second box is only for reissued prints; leave it empty if there are none.

Headcodes

Only Gillingham, Victoria and Grove Park carry a headcode as standard. Tick a book to put one on every line of it — empty moves and platform starters alike. The books rebuild as you tick, so save them again afterwards.

What the tool does with the prints

It reads each diagram's day from the prints, merges any reissues over the originals, and builds a workbook for each fleet in the house format — Mainline, Metro and High Speed. Use Look at it on each book to see the finished sheet on screen before saving. When a reissue has been merged into prints that came in as a Word file, an updated prints document is offered as well — there is nothing to splice a reissue into if the prints were pasted, and the review list says so.

If a weekend build looks wrong
"That file is damaged or isn't a Word document"
Open the prints in Word and save them again as .docx, then drop the new file.
"That isn't the diagram prints"
Pasted or plain-text prints whose tabs have been lost. The diagram lines are held together by them, so the page refuses rather than half-reading it. Copy the document out of Word again and paste it as it comes, without tidying it up.
Can the Genius or Integrale CSVs build a weekend?
No. Saturday and Sunday books come from the diagram prints and nothing else — this panel reads the prints whatever the file is called, Word document or plain text, but it cannot read a Diagram Summary or Diagram Detail export. A weekend-dated pair dropped on the weekday panel above says so rather than building an empty book.
A book says "skipped"
No diagrams for that fleet exist in this weekend's prints — nothing to build, nothing wrong.
What comes back

The books

  • SHEETS — the mainline book (375 / 376 / 377 units), every section from Ashford to West Marina.
  • RAM_SHEETS — Ramsgate's own book, cut out of the mainline day. Ramsgate has its own depot and its own people, so its section is lifted into a book of its own rather than printed with the rest.
  • METRO_SHEETS — the metro book (465 / 466 / 707 units).
  • HS_SHEETS — the High Speed book (395 units), built whenever the reports contain High Speed diagrams.
  • Weekend — Mainline, Metro and High Speed workbooks, one per fleet, plus the updated prints when a reissue was merged.

Before anything goes out

Read the review list on every build. Anything the rules had to decide for themselves is named there — locations that needed looking up, empty moves left off, diagrams whose pages didn't read cleanly, a unit order recorded somewhere but not here. Nothing is quietly guessed at.

Always double-checkThese are drafting tools, and mistakes and bad uploads happen — check every book against the reports, the prints and the ACWNs before it goes out.

Everything the tool needs — the report and prints readers, the berthing rulebook and the workbook writer — is built into this one file, running in the browser on this computer. No report, print or sheet leaves the machine, and the page works without an internet connection.
