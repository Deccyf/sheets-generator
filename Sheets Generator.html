Fleet & depot · unit berthing

Berthing sheetsthe week's books, built from the plans

This page turns the planning paperwork into the unit berthing books. Weekday books are built from the Diagram Summary and Detail reports — from Genius or from Integrale; Saturday and Sunday sheets from the weekend diagram prints. The page reads the dropped files, works out where every unit berths and ends its day, and writes the books in the house format — diagram numbers, AM and PM columns, flags and notes filled in — ready to check on screen and save.

Quick start

  1. Get the paperwork as files. For a weekday: the Diagram Summary and Diagram Detail reports for the date — from Genius as PDFs, or from Integrale as the two CSV exports. For the weekend: the diagram prints Word document(s). In Genius, a Control Cycle must be created before the Diagram Summary and Diagram Detail reports can be run — and if the Metro and High Speed sheets are wanted, Control Cycles must be created for those fleets too.
    Where to find the reports in Genius In Genius: Diagrams → Summary Report… then Diagrams → Detail Report… — save each as .pdf for the date.
    Getting the two CSVs out of Integrale First, the Diagram Summary
    1. Open the Stock Diagrams list and click [GO→] without selecting a filter.
    2. Click [Export To Excel].
    3. Save it in a sensible folder under a sensible name, with File Type set to .csv, then click [Save].
    Then the Diagram Detail
    1. Open the Stock Diagrams list and click [GO→] again, still without a filter.
    2. Click Stock Diagram Detail Report on the toolbar.
    3. The dialog opens at the bottom of the screen — click […] under Output Path.
    4. Pick the same folder as the Summary, give it a sensible name, File Type .csv again, then [Save].
    5. Click [OK] — you may have to scroll down to see it. It takes a minute or so, then tells you it is done.
    That is the pair. Drop them both on the weekday panel below.
  2. Drop them on the matching panel below — weekday PDFs on the first panel, weekend prints on the second. Or click a panel to browse for the files.
  3. Look the sheets over on screen, then save. Every build shows a preview and a review list of anything that needs a human eye before the books go out.
Weekdays · Mon – Fri

Daily sheets from the Genius reports

Needs both reports for the date — the Diagram Summary (one line per diagram) and the Diagram Detail (every stop of every diagram) — from Genius as PDFs or CSVs, or from Integrale as its two CSV exports. Drop the pair together; a Monday pair builds the Monday books.

Headcodes

Put every headcode in the notes column — empty moves and platform starters alike, Victoria & Grove Park style. Left off, each book keeps the house rules.

What the tool does with these reports

It reads every diagram's day from the Detail report, works out where each unit berths, and writes the entries the way the hand-built books do: departure times off the platform where the working runs through it, units listed rear-first with their three-digit diagram numbers, AM and PM columns showing where each unit goes next and where it ends its day, SPLITS and SPLITS PM flags, siding notes such as EAST SIDINGS, ATTACHMENT notes, and the end markers at Dover Priory, Folkestone East, Hastings and West Marina.

Empty moves to a berth with no passenger work afterwards are left off, the same as the hand-built sheets — each one is named on the review list so nothing disappears silently. A move that takes a unit out of its section, though, is the unit going away for the night and is printed: the section it leaves has to show it going.

It also tests each working against the few Sectional Appendix rules the reports can actually settle — formation make-up, who may berth on a road, route clearance and the 12-car limits — and anything that breaks one goes on the review list marked as the Appendix's rule rather than the tool's. That is a prompt to go and look the rule up, not a substitute for the Appendix.

If something looks wrong
"No Diagram Summary rows found" / "No Diagram Detail itineraries found"
One of the two reports is missing or the wrong kind. Both must be dropped, saved for the same date — both from Genius (PDF or CSV, and one of each is fine) or both from Integrale. A Genius report paired with an Integrale export won't build.
A location comes up under its own heading
A unit berthed somewhere the section list doesn't know. It gets its own section in alphabetical order and a note on the review list — check it, and say if it should live under an existing section instead.
A train is missing
Check the review list first: empty moves to a berth are left off deliberately and each one is named there. If it isn't listed, report the diagram number so the rule can be traced.
Weekend · Sat & Sun

Saturday & Sunday sheets from the diagram prints

Needs the weekend diagram prints Word document (.docx or .doc). If reissued prints exist, drop them too — together or in a later drop — and the reissued diagrams replace their originals before anything is built.

Headcodes

Put every headcode in the notes column — empty moves and platform starters alike, Victoria & Grove Park style. Left off, each book keeps the house rules.

What the tool does with the prints

It reads each diagram's day from the prints, merges any reissues over the originals, and builds a workbook for each fleet in the house format — Mainline, Metro and High Speed. Use Look at it on each book to see the finished sheet on screen before saving. When a reissue has been merged, an updated prints document is offered as well.

The Sectional Appendix checks run on the weekday panel only — the prints do not carry the class and formation detail those rules are tested against. The Appendix applies just the same on a Saturday, so check a weekend book against it yourself.

If something looks wrong
"That file is damaged or isn't a Word document"
Open the prints in Word and save them again as .docx, then drop the new file.
A book says "skipped"
No diagrams for that fleet exist in this weekend's prints — nothing to build, nothing wrong.
What comes back

The books

  • SHEETS — the mainline book (375 / 376 / 377 units), every section from Ashford to West Marina.
  • RAM_SHEETS — Ramsgate's own book, cut from the same day's workings.
  • METRO_SHEETS — the metro book (465 / 466 / 707 units).
  • HS_SHEETS — the High Speed book (395 units), built whenever the reports contain High Speed diagrams.
  • Weekend — Mainline, Metro and High Speed workbooks, one per fleet, plus the updated prints when a reissue was merged.

Before anything goes out

Read the review list on every build. It carries two kinds of item and they are not the same claim. Most are the tool showing its working — locations that needed looking up, empty moves left off, diagrams whose pages didn't read cleanly; nothing is quietly guessed at. The ones marked Sectional Appendix are the other kind: a published document says the working is not permitted, and those are worth taking back to the Appendix itself before the book goes out.

Always double-checkThese are drafting tools, and mistakes and bad uploads happen — check every book against the reports, the prints and the ACWNs before it goes out.

Everything the tool needs — the report and prints readers, the berthing rulebook and the workbook writer — is built into this one file, running in the browser on this computer. No report, print or sheet leaves the machine, and the page works without an internet connection.
