Berthing sheets

Drop the day's planning paperwork below and the berthing books come back built — checked on screen, then saved. Nothing leaves this computer.

Version 3.1.0 · 4 September 2026
How to use this page

Quick start

  1. Get the paperwork as files. For a weekday: the Diagram Summary and Diagram Detail reports for the date, from Genius as PDFs or from Integrale as its two CSV exports. For the weekend: the diagram prints Word document, or the same text. In Genius, a Control Cycle must exist before the reports can be run — one for each fleet you want a book for.
    Where to find the reports in Genius Diagrams › Summary Report… then Diagrams › Detail Report… — save each as .pdf for the date.
    Getting the two CSVs out of Integrale First, the Diagram Summary
    1. Open the Stock Diagrams list and click [GO→] without selecting a filter.
    2. Click [Export To Excel].
    3. Save it in a sensible folder under a sensible name, with File Type set to .csv, then click [Save].
    Then the Diagram Detail (Integrale calls it Diagrams)
    1. Open the Stock Diagrams list and click [GO→] again, still without a filter.
    2. Click Stock Diagram Detail Report on the toolbar.
    3. Pick the same folder as the Summary, give it a sensible name, File Type .csv again, then [Save].
    4. Click [OK] — you may have to scroll down to see it. It takes a minute or so, then tells you it is done.
    That is the pair. Drop them both on the Weekday panel.
  2. Drop them on the panel. Pick Weekday or Weekend, then drop the files on the zone — or click it to browse. Files that belong on the other panel are sent there for you. A machine that will not let the files be saved anywhere the browser can reach can have the paperwork pasted in instead; the one thing that cannot be pasted is a PDF.
  3. Look the books over, then save. Every book shows its sheet on screen, exactly as it will print — A4 portrait, all eight columns on one page across, no location cut in half, page breaks marked — with a Review tab of anything that needs a human eye before it goes out.

What comes back

  • SHEETS — the Mainline book (375 / 376 / 377 units), every section from Ashford to West Marina.
  • RAM_SHEETS — Ramsgate's own book, cut out of the mainline day for Ramsgate's own people.
  • METRO_SHEETS — the Metro book (465 / 466 / 707 units). Not a berthing sheet: the depot's own Metro document, a worksheet per location, landscape, with POS, S, ENDS and MILES and five columns left ruled and empty to fill in by hand.
  • HS_SHEETS — the High Speed book (395 units). Not a berthing sheet either: the Class 395 Allocations Sheet, a worksheet per day with a block for each depot, last night's arrivals beside today's allocations.
  • STOCK_REQUIREMENTS — the Kent Coast stock requirements form, when the option is ticked: every diagram counted at the location it starts the day from.
  • Weekend — the same four books from the prints: Mainline and Ramsgate as berthing sheets, Metro and High Speed as the depot's own documents, plus the updated prints document when a reissue was spliced in.

The full rulebook — every rule a book followed — is on the Rules tab of that book, and in BERTHING SHEET RULES for circulating. Both are written out from the tool's own tables, so neither can fall behind what the books actually do.

If a build looks wrong

"No Diagram Summary rows found" / "No Diagram Detail itineraries found"
One of the two reports is missing or the wrong kind. Both must be for the same date, and both from Genius or both from Integrale.
A location comes up under its own heading
A unit berthed somewhere the section list does not know. It gets its own section, in alphabetical order, and a note on the Review tab — check it, and say if it should live under an existing section.
A train is missing
Read the Review tab first: empty moves to a berth are left off on purpose and each one is named there. If it is not listed, report the diagram number so the rule can be traced.
"That file is damaged or isn't a Word document"
Open the prints in Word and save them again as .docx, then drop the new file.
"That isn't the diagram prints"
Pasted or plain-text prints whose columns were lost — the tabs flattened to spaces. Copy the document out of Word again and paste it as it comes, without tidying it. A .csv of the prints is fine.
A weekend card says "No … diagrams in this weekend's prints"
No diagrams for that fleet exist in the prints — nothing to build, nothing wrong.

Weekday books from the planning reports

Needs both reports for the date — the Diagram Summary and the Diagram Detail — from Genius (.pdf or .csv) or from Integrale (its two CSV exports). A Monday pair builds the Monday books.

Before you exportIn Genius, File › Session Settings, tick “Show diagram sections on the diagram summary report”. Without it the Summary carries only the morning positions, afternoon formations print the wrong way round, and it cannot be corrected later. The Review tab says if this happened.

Open each CSV in Notepad or Excel, select all, copy, and paste it into its box — both reports, for the same date. Integrale calls the second one Diagrams. PDFs cannot be pasted; drop those as files. One at a time is fine: paste one and drop the other. Can’t paste at all? Drag the selected rows, or the CSV file itself, straight into the box.

Options

Headcodes on every line — only Gillingham, Victoria and Grove Park carry one as standard; this puts one on every line of the Mainline and Ramsgate books, empty moves and platform starters alike. Metro and High Speed already carry theirs.

Count long platform stands — a unit standing in a platform for an hour or more gets a line on the sheet; otherwise it is named on the Review tab and left off.

Mileage column — adds MILES to the Mainline and Ramsgate books: the miles a unit runs from that departure until it next berths on the book. A unit that attaches to another diagram and stays out has no second row, so the row it does have carries the rest of its day. Blank on a build from PDFs, which carry no mileage.

Stock requirements form — fills in the Kent Coast form, every diagram counted at the location it starts the day from, by unit type. POSITION and SEAT LOSS stay blank for the planner. Normally only wanted for a Monday built on a Sunday night, or the day after a bank holiday.

The options are remembered on this computer, and every one rebuilds the books as you tick it — save them again afterwards.

Weekend books from the diagram prints

Needs the weekend diagram prints — the Word document (.docx or .doc), or the same saved as text or as a CSV. Drop reissued prints too, together or in a later drop, and the reissued diagrams replace their originals before anything is built.

In Word, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C and paste into the first box. The diagram lines keep their columns as tabs, so paste as it comes — do not tidy it. A comma-separated save from a spreadsheet is read too. Can’t paste at all? Drag the selection or the file straight into the box. The second box is only for reissued prints.

Headcodes on every line Only Gillingham, Victoria and Grove Park carry a headcode as standard. Ticking a book puts one on every line of it and rebuilds it — save again afterwards.

Always double-checkThese are drafting tools. Check every book against the reports, the prints and the ACWNs before it goes out — and read its Review tab: anything the rules had to decide for themselves is named there, nothing is quietly guessed at.
